Wedding season in Dothan has always kept local salons busy, but several beauty professionals in the city say the nature of bridal bookings has changed in recent years, shifting from single-day service requests toward structured, multi-day preparation packages that begin well before the wedding itself.

At Wiregrass Bridal Beauty, a small studio that works almost exclusively with wedding clients, owner Sasha Kellerman said the change reflects brides doing more advance planning generally. "Nobody wants to try a new skincare routine for the first time the week of their wedding," she said. "We're building relationships with brides months out now instead of just showing up the morning of."

## A Longer Timeline for Bridal Preparation

Rather than a single appointment for hair and makeup on the wedding day, several Dothan salons now offer structured timelines that begin as early as three to six months before the event, including trial runs for both hair and makeup, skincare consultations aimed at addressing any concerns well before the wedding date, and a final pre-wedding appointment in the week leading up to the event for last-minute touch-ups.

This extended approach allows time to address issues that might otherwise cause stress close to the wedding date, such as reactions to new skincare products or dissatisfaction with a trial hairstyle. Beauty professionals say the additional lead time has reduced the number of last-minute changes requested on wedding mornings, when there is little room for error.

## Coordinating Wedding-Day Logistics

Wedding-day service itself has also become more logistically involved, with several Dothan beauty teams now offering on-location service at wedding venues rather than requiring the bridal party to travel to a salon. This shift accommodates the reality that many area weddings take place at venues outside the city center, including rural event spaces and family properties throughout the Wiregrass region, where salon access on the wedding morning would otherwise be impractical.

Coordinating hair and makeup for an entire bridal party, sometimes including a dozen or more attendants, requires careful scheduling to ensure everyone is ready in time for photos, which typically begin well before the ceremony itself. Some beauty teams have adopted detailed timeline documents shared with brides in advance, mapping out exactly when each person in the party will be served.

## Skincare as Part of the Bridal Timeline

Skincare has become a more explicit part of bridal preparation packages, with several Dothan estheticians offering bridal-specific facial series designed to be completed on a schedule that avoids irritation close to the wedding date, since certain treatments can cause temporary redness or peeling if performed too soon before an event. Estheticians generally recommend scheduling any new or more intensive treatments no later than a month before the wedding, reserving the final pre-wedding appointment for a gentle, low-risk treatment only.

## A Reflection of Broader Planning Trends

The shift toward longer bridal beauty timelines mirrors broader trends in wedding planning generally, as couples increasingly research and book vendors further in advance. Beauty professionals in Dothan describe the change as ultimately reducing stress for both clients and providers, since a longer runway allows for adjustments that would be far more difficult to manage under the time pressure of a single wedding-day appointment.
