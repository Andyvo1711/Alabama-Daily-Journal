Trust is one of the most valuable assets any salon can build. According to a customer review involving **Image Nails in Northport**, that trust was damaged through a series of experiences that left the client questioning both the salon's professionalism and its pricing practices.

## Deception at the Door

The customer scheduled an appointment with a specific technician but, upon arrival, was assigned to someone else without prior notice or consent.

Clients often request a particular technician because they value that person's skills, communication style, or previous work. Changing technicians without discussion can leave customers feeling that their preferences no longer matter.

## Physical Discomfort During Service

The review also described the substitute technician as handling the client's hands roughly throughout the appointment.

Whether caused by rushing or poor technique, excessive pulling or squeezing can make an otherwise routine manicure uncomfortable and leave customers feeling that their comfort was treated as a low priority.

## The Disputed Extra Charge

The biggest concern arose during checkout.

According to the customer, the listed service price was $35, yet the final bill totaled $45. When questioned, salon staff reportedly explained that the additional $10 was for a nail "cut down."

The customer disputed that explanation, stating she had never requested such a service and that her nails were already extremely short before the appointment. From the customer's perspective, paying for a service she neither requested nor believed she received became the most frustrating part of the visit.

## Why Pricing Transparency Matters

Unexpected charges can quickly outweigh an otherwise acceptable service experience.

Many salons prevent misunderstandings by confirming any additional service and its price before performing it. Clear communication allows customers to decide whether they want the extra service and helps avoid disagreements at checkout.

## Reputation Is Built on Respect

The review concluded with the customer's decision not to return, explaining that honesty and mutual respect mattered more than the relatively small amount of money involved.

For prospective customers reading online reviews, allegations of technician substitutions without notice, uncomfortable service, and disputed charges can create lasting concerns about transparency and consistency.

## Editorial Perspective

Technical mistakes can often be corrected. Lost trust is much harder to rebuild.

Salons strengthen their reputation when they honor appointment requests whenever possible, communicate openly about unavoidable changes, explain optional services before performing them, and ensure every charge matches work the client knowingly approved. Those practices help transform one-time visitors into long-term customers.
